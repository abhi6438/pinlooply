🚀 Introducing **Pinlooply** — a modern project management & team collaboration tool built for the way real teams work.

Managing projects across different teams, roles, and workflows has always been messy. Pinlooply brings it all together in one clean, fast workspace.

**Here's what you can do with Pinlooply:**

✅ **Projects & Tasks** — Kanban boards, task assignments, custom statuses, and priority tracking
📅 **Timeline** — Visual Gantt-style planning to keep your team aligned
💬 **Topics & Discussions** — Capture decisions, notes, and context in one place
📋 **Standup & Summary** — Auto-generate daily standups and weekly reports powered by AI
🧪 **Test Cases** — Built-in QA management, no separate tool needed
👥 **Team Management** — Invite teammates, assign roles (owner, admin, member), and control access at every level
🔒 **Role-based Menu Access** — Admins and team owners can control exactly which features each member sees
🎨 **Fully Customizable** — Rename labels, choose your profession workflow, set custom colors

Whether you're a solo builder, a startup, or a growing team — Pinlooply adapts to your workflow, not the other way around.

🔗 **Try it free:** https://pinlooply.vercel.app/
